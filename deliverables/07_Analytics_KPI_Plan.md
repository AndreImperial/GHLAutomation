# Analytics, KPI Framework, and Optimization Plan: Bloom Dental Studio

## 1. North Star Metric

Cost per booked consultation.

Formula:

`Total ad spend / booked consultations`

Why it matters:

The campaign is designed to create qualified appointments. Whitening revenue depends on booked and completed consultations.

## 2. KPI Targets

Booked consultations:

60 in 60 days

No-show rate:

Reduce from 35% to below 15%

Whitening attach rate:

40% of completed consultations

## 3. Funnel Metrics

### Traffic

- Meta impressions
- Meta clicks
- Click-through rate
- Landing page visits

### Lead Capture

- Form submissions
- Landing page conversion rate

Formula:

`Form submissions / landing page visits`

### Booking

- Booked consultations
- Lead-to-booking rate

Formula:

`Booked consultations / form submissions`

### Attendance

- Completed consultations
- No-shows
- No-show rate

Formula:

`No-shows / booked consultations`

### Revenue

- Whitening bookings
- Whitening attach rate
- Whitening revenue

Formula:

`Whitening bookings / completed consultations`

Revenue formula:

`Whitening bookings x PHP 15,000`

## 5. Weekly Optimization Logic

If clicks are low:

Improve ad hook, creative, and audience.

If landing page conversion is low:

Improve headline, proof, pricing clarity, CTA, and form length.

If lead-to-booking rate is low:

Improve form-to-calendar handoff and booking follow-up.

If no-show rate is high:

Improve reminder timing, SMS delivery, address clarity, and reschedule links.

If whitening attach rate is low:

Improve post-consultation offer, in-person handoff, price clarity, and follow-up timing.
