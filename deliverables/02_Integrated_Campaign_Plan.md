# Integrated Campaign Plan: Bloom Dental Studio

## 1. Campaign Overview

Campaign name:

Bloom Dental Studio - 60-Day New Patient Consultation Campaign

Campaign type:

Lead generation and appointment booking campaign.

Primary channel:

Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram.

Primary destination:

GHL landing page with New Patient Inquiry form and booking calendar.

Primary offer:

Free 30-minute new-patient consultation.

Secondary offer:

Premium In-Chair Whitening at PHP 15,000 per session.

## 2. SMART Goal

Book 60 confirmed new-patient consultations within 60 days through a dedicated GHL landing page, inquiry form, and automated booking/reminder workflow.

Success metric:

Confirmed appointments in the Bloom Dental GHL calendar.

## 3. Audience

Primary persona:

Busy BGC/Makati professional, age 28-45, appearance-conscious, income-stable, and interested in improving their smile.

Main objections:

- "I do not have time."
- "I do not want to be pressured."
- "I want to know what this costs."
- "I need to trust the clinic first."

## 4. Campaign Message

Book a free 30-minute consultation to understand your smile options without pressure or surprise pricing.

## 5. Channel Roles

Meta ads:

Drive cold and retargeted traffic to the GHL landing page.

Landing page:

Explain the free consultation offer and reduce trust, time, and price concerns.

GHL form:

Capture contact details, service interest, and SMS consent.

Calendar:

Convert interest into a booked appointment.

Email:

Confirm bookings, remind patients, and explain next steps.

SMS:

Send fast appointment reminders and no-show recovery messages.

Workflow:

Move contacts through the correct stages and trigger the right messages.

Dashboard:

Track whether the campaign is producing booked consultations and whitening revenue.

## 6. Campaign Flow

1. Meta ad click
2. Landing page visit
3. Form submission
4. Calendar booking
5. Booking confirmation
6. 24-hour reminder
7. 2-hour reminder
8. Appointment completed or no-show
9. Whitening upsell or no-show recovery
10. Recall / future nurture

## 7. Required Assets

- Meta ad angle
- Landing page copy
- Inquiry form
- Booking calendar
- CRM pipeline
- Tags and custom fields
- Email templates
- SMS snippets
- Workflow automation
- Analytics dashboard

## 8. Campaign Risks

Risk:

Leads submit the form but do not book.

Response:

Redirect immediately to calendar and send booking link by email/SMS.

Risk:

Booked consultations do not show.

Response:

Use 24-hour email and 2-hour SMS reminders.

Risk:

Completed consultations do not convert to whitening.

Response:

Send same-day whitening follow-up with clear price and booking link.
