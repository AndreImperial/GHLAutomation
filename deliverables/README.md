# Deliverables

The eight project deliverables and two filled workshop templates from the Bloom Dental case study by Andre Imperial. Each one is also readable in full on the [live site](https://ghlautomation.onrender.com/#deliverables).

Bloom Dental Studio is a fictional clinic from the Libre Academy Fast Track Workshop. The campaign was never launched, so every performance figure is a projected goal.

| # | Document | What it covers | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Marketing Strategy Document | Clinic, audience, problem, positioning, funnel recommendation, KPIs | [Markdown](01_Marketing_Strategy_Document.md) · [PDF](pdf/01_Marketing_Strategy_Document.pdf) |
| 02 | Integrated Campaign Plan | Channel roles, goals, and the campaign operating plan | [Markdown](02_Integrated_Campaign_Plan.md) · [PDF](pdf/02_Integrated_Campaign_Plan.pdf) |
| 03 | Funnel Map and GHL Blueprint | Forms, calendar, pipeline stages, tags, and custom fields | [Markdown](03_Funnel_Map_and_GHL_Blueprint.md) · [PDF](pdf/03_Funnel_Map_and_GHL_Blueprint.pdf) |
| 04 | Landing Page Copy Deck | Offer, objections, benefits, FAQs, and calls to action | [Markdown](04_Landing_Page_Copy_Deck.md) · [PDF](pdf/04_Landing_Page_Copy_Deck.pdf) |
| 05 | Email and SMS Sequence | The 9-message confirmation, reminder, recovery, and follow-up sequence | [Markdown](05_Email_SMS_Sequence.md) · [PDF](pdf/05_Email_SMS_Sequence.pdf) |
| 06 | GHL Workflow Specification | Triggers, steps, branches, and stop rules for the six workflows | [Markdown](06_GHL_Workflow_Specification.md) · [PDF](pdf/06_GHL_Workflow_Specification.pdf) |
| 07 | Analytics and KPI Plan | North star metric, funnel formulas, dashboard, weekly review | [Markdown](07_Analytics_KPI_Plan.md) · [PDF](pdf/07_Analytics_KPI_Plan.pdf) |
| 08 | GHL Build Checklist | The paste-ready build list for the GHL account | [Markdown](08_GHL_Build_Checklist_Paste_Ready.md) · [PDF](pdf/08_GHL_Build_Checklist_Paste_Ready.pdf) |
| T1 | Business Case Intake Worksheet (filled) | The completed pre-workshop brief | [PDF](templates/WS_00_Business_Case_Intake_Worksheet_FILLED_Bloom_Dental.pdf) |
| T2 | KPI Scorecard (filled) | Campaign setup, weekly tracker, and dashboard formulas | [Workbook](templates/WS_08_KPI_Scorecard_FILLED_Bloom_Dental.xlsx) |
