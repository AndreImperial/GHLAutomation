11 / DATA MODEL

The system remembers the right things at the right level.

Good automation depends on clean distinctions between a person, an opportunity, an appointment, and a message event.

WHAT GHL STORESRecord > stage > event
CONTACTPerson recordName, email, phone, service interest, SMS consent, UTM source.
OPPORTUNITYBusiness progressNew Lead, Booked Consultation, Consultation Complete, Whitening Booked, Whitening Paid, Lost.
EVENTSWhat happenedForm submitted, appointment booked, completed, no-show, payment, recall due.
WHY THE MODEL MATTERS

If these concepts are mixed together, the system cannot answer simple questions such as: Did this person book? Where did the opportunity stop?

  • Fields preserve context.
  • Tags create reusable signals.
  • Pipeline stages create a visible business state.
  • Events make the funnel measurable.

13 / WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

Six smaller workflows make the journey testable.

Each automation owns one behavior, with its own trigger, wait, message, stage change, branch, handoff, and stop condition.

DESIGN DECISION

Modular workflows are easier to explain, troubleshoot, and improve than one giant sequence that hides its branches.

  • Lead capture owns the invitation to book.
  • Booking owns confirmation and appointment protection.
  • Outcome workflows own recovery, follow-up, payment, and recall.
14 / WORKFLOW DEEP DIVE

EXPLAIN THE NODES
  • Trigger: form submitted.
  • Actions: add bd-new-lead, create/update the opportunity, and save service interest.
  • Consent: add the SMS opt-in signal and send SMS only when permission exists.
  • Wait: give the lead 24 hours to book, then remind only if the appointment is still missing.
  • Exit: appointment booked ends the unfinished booking path.
15 / OUTCOME WORKFLOWS

The system continues without chasing forever.

After booking, the automation responds to real outcomes: attended, missed, interested, paid, or due for future care.

THE SIX-WORKFLOW OPERATING MODELOutcome > next responsible path
BOOKEDConfirm, remind, then route completed or no-show.
NO-SHOWRebook with time-boxed email, SMS, and Lost exit.
COMPLETEDOffer relevant whitening education without claiming a sale.
PAIDMove to Whitening Paid and start the retention timer.
RECALLWait six months, remind with consent, stop on booking.
WHY THE BRANCHES EXIST

The workflow map makes every branch inspectable. A condition is not just a technical step; it protects the patient experience and the business record.

  • Completed appointments must not enter no-show recovery.
  • Booked whitening must not receive another promotional sequence.
  • Opt-outs and missing consent must stop the relevant channel.

WEEKLY REVIEW

Diagnose before optimizing.

Cadence

  1. Check volume and delivery health.
  2. Compare each stage to its prior week.
  3. Find the first meaningful drop.
  4. Change one variable and record it.

Diagnostic playbook

  • Clicks high, form submits low: clarify the offer or reduce friction.
  • Leads high, bookings low: inspect the calendar handoff and message timing.
  • Bookings high, shows low: review reminder content, timing, and availability.
  • Consults high, whitening low: inspect fit, education, and the interest field.

Glossary

GoHighLevel / GHL
A marketing CRM used to capture leads, book appointments, send messages, and move people through pipeline stages.
Funnel
The step-by-step path from seeing an ad to booking, attending, and receiving follow-up.
Lead
A person who showed interest by clicking, filling out a form, or asking for an appointment.
Pipeline
The CRM path that tracks lead status from New Lead to Whitening Paid or Lost.
Tag
A label used to trigger logic or mark behavior, such as SMS opt-in or no-show.
No-show recovery
A follow-up path that helps missed appointments rebook instead of disappearing.
Attach rate
The share of completed consultations that convert into whitening bookings.
North Star metric
The main metric for judging the campaign: cost per booked consultation.

Reflection

This project taught me to think about a campaign as an operating system, not a single asset. The landing page matters, but it only works when the offer, form, calendar, reminders, CRM stages, consent rules, recovery paths, and KPI loop are designed together.

The hardest part was keeping the simulation honest: the campaign needed targets and measurement logic, but it could not pretend to have real after-data. That is why the site separates designed future state from live performance.

Before a live launch, I would add verified clinic proof, finish sender configuration, pass every workflow branch with test contacts, connect Meta traffic, and replace projected targets with actual weekly performance data.

Portfolio positioning

A working proof piece for strategy, funnel thinking, and GHL implementation.

This project is strongest when presented as a system: business diagnosis, campaign plan, funnel blueprint, copy assets, implemented GHL objects, workflow logic, and measurement plan. That gives reviewers a clear view of both the thinking and the hands-on build, while keeping simulated targets separate from results.
