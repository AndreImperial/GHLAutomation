Marketing and GHL glossary

Marketing
Creating and moving demand by matching a problem, audience, offer, and channel.
Funnel
A measurable sequence of conversion steps and drop-offs.

Attribution
The method used to connect a result to its source or campaign touchpoint.

11 / DATA MODEL

The system remembers the right things at the right level.

Good automation depends on clean distinctions between a person, an opportunity, an appointment, and a message event.

WHAT THE SYSTEM REMEMBERSPerson > place > change
PERSONWho Sam isName, contact details, needs, permission, and where Sam came from.
PLACEWhere Sam isNew, booked, finished, interested, paid, or done.
CHANGEWhat happenedAsked, chose a time, attended, paid, or became due for care.
WHY THE MODEL MATTERS

If these concepts are mixed together, the system cannot answer simple questions such as: Did this person book? Where did the opportunity stop?

  • Fields preserve context.
  • Tags create reusable signals.
  • Pipeline stages create a visible business state.
  • Events make the funnel measurable.

13 / WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

Six smaller workflows make the journey testable.

Each automation owns one behavior, with its own trigger, wait, message, stage change, branch, handoff, and stop condition.

01Help a new person choose a time02Confirm and remind03Help after a missed visit04Follow up after the visit05Record completed payment06Remember future care
Every helper knows when to stop
DESIGN DECISION

Modular workflows are easier to explain, troubleshoot, and improve than one giant sequence that hides its branches.

  • Lead capture owns the invitation to book.
  • Booking owns confirmation and appointment protection.
  • Outcome workflows own recovery, follow-up, payment, and recall.
14 / WORKFLOW DEEP DIVE

A new lead becomes a bookable, trackable opportunity.

This is the most important early sequence because it connects the form submission to the calendar without losing context or consent.

ONE HELPER / FIVE SMALL STEPSNotice > remember > invite > wait > check
YES stop after booking NO send one reminder
EXPLAIN THE NODES
  • Trigger: form submitted.
  • Actions: add bd-new-lead, create/update the opportunity, and save service interest.
  • Consent: add the SMS opt-in signal and send SMS only when permission exists.
  • Wait: give the lead 24 hours to book, then remind only if the appointment is still missing.
  • Exit: appointment booked ends the unfinished booking path.
15 / OUTCOME WORKFLOWS

The system continues without chasing forever.

After booking, the automation responds to real outcomes: attended, missed, interested, paid, or due for future care.

THE SIX AUTOMATIC HELPERSWhat happened > helpful next action
CHOSENConfirm the time and send reminders.
MISSEDOffer another time, then stop.
FINISHEDSend only the follow-up that fits.
PAIDSave the outcome for future care.
DUE LATERRemind about a future check-up.
WHY THE BRANCHES EXIST

The workflow map makes every branch inspectable. A condition is not just a technical step; it protects the patient experience and the business record.

  • Completed appointments must not enter no-show recovery.
  • Booked whitening must not receive another promotional sequence.
  • Opt-outs and missing consent must stop the relevant channel.
16 / MEASUREMENT

Targets become useful only when they are measurable.

The dashboard plan connects funnel events to decisions. It does not present simulated goals as achieved results.

PROJECTED GOAL60bookings in 60 days
PROJECTED GUARDRAIL<15%no-show rate
PROJECTED VALUE PATH40%whitening attach rate
Count steps > ask questions > run one test
MEASUREMENT LOGIC

Start with events, then derive rates. Do not jump from a target number to a conclusion.

  • Booking rate = booked consultations / qualified leads
  • No-show rate = no-shows / booked consultations
  • Whitening attach = whitening booked / completed consultations

Marketing Strategy Document: Bloom Dental Studio

1. Executive Summary

Bloom Dental Studio is a cosmetic and general dentistry clinic in Bonifacio Global City serving busy professionals in the BGC and Makati corridor. The clinic already receives some inbound interest, but its follow-up, booking, reminders, and post-consultation conversion process are too manual.

The primary campaign opportunity is to use GoHighLevel to turn interested prospects into booked consultations, reduce missed appointments, and create a structured path from consultation completion to Premium In-Chair Whitening revenue.

Primary campaign goal:

Projected simulation goal: Book 60 confirmed free new-patient consultations within 60 days.

Supporting projected goals:

  • Reduce no-show rate from 35% to below 15%.
  • Convert 40% of completed consultations into Premium In-Chair Whitening bookings.
  • Build a repeatable GHL system for lead capture, appointment booking, reminders, no-show recovery, and post-consultation upsell.

Business:

Bloom Dental Studio

  • Six-month cleaning and check-up

Entry offer:

Free 30-minute new-patient consultation.

Premium In-Chair Whitening at PHP 15,000 per session.

Retention offer:

Six-month cleaning and check-up at PHP 3,500 per visit.

3. Target Audience

The primary audience is appearance-conscious professionals aged roughly 28-45 who work in BGC, Makati, finance, tech, corporate, and similar fields.

They are:

  • Busy and time-poor
  • Comfortable investing in appearance
  • Interested in a better smile
  • Cautious about price uncertainty
  • Sensitive to hard-selling
  • Likely to check reviews and proof before booking

Their main hesitation is not only money. It is also trust, time, and fear of pressure.

4. Current State

Workshop scenario inputs, not observed campaign results:

Current lead flow:

  • Around 20 website leads per month
  • Around 6 become booked appointments
  • Approximate lead-to-booking rate: 30%
  • No-show rate: 35%

Current problems:

  • Follow-up is handled manually by the front desk.
  • Leads can sit for one or two days before response.
  • Appointment reminders are inconsistent.
  • No-show recovery is not systemized.
  • Post-consultation whitening follow-up is informal.
  • Six-month recall is manual.

5. Strategic Diagnosis

Bloom Dental does not mainly have an awareness problem. It has a conversion and follow-up problem.

The system should therefore focus on:

  • Faster speed-to-lead
  • Easier booking
  • Stronger appointment reminders
  • Automated no-show recovery
  • Post-consultation whitening follow-up
  • Longer-term recall automation

6. Recommended Funnel Strategy

Use a dedicated GHL landing page connected to a GHL form and booking calendar.

The front-end offer should be the free 30-minute consultation, not whitening.

Why:

  • The consultation is lower friction for cold traffic.
  • It builds trust before the PHP 15,000 whitening offer.
  • It gives the clinic a chance to recommend treatment based on patient goals.
  • It creates a measurable conversion event in GHL.

8. Workflow A - New Lead To Booking

Trigger:

Form submitted: Bloom Dental - New Patient Inquiry

Actions:

  1. Add tag bd-new-lead.
  2. Add or update opportunity in New Lead.
  3. If service interest = Whitening, add tag bd-whitening-interest.
  4. Send email with booking link.
  5. If SMS consent is true, send SMS with booking link.
  6. Wait 24 hours.
  7. If no appointment is booked, send reminder to book.

9. Workflow B - Appointment Show-Up

Trigger:

Appointment Status = Booked

Filter:

Calendar = Bloom Dental - Free Consultation

Actions:

  1. Add tag bd-consult-booked.
  2. Move opportunity to Booked Consultation.
  3. Send booking confirmation SMS.
  4. Send booking confirmation email.
  5. Wait until 24 hours before appointment.
  6. Send 24-hour reminder email.
  7. Wait until 2 hours before appointment.
  8. Send 2-hour reminder SMS.
  9. Wait until after appointment time.
  10. Check appointment status.

If completed:

  • Move opportunity to Consultation Complete.
  • Add tag bd-consult-complete.
  • Start Workflow D - Post-Consultation Upsell.

If no-show:

  • Add tag bd-no-show.
  • Move opportunity to Lost or no-show substage if available.
  • Start Workflow C - No-Show Recovery.

10. Workflow C - No-Show Recovery

Trigger:

Appointment Status = No-Show

Actions:

  1. Send SMS: friendly rebook prompt.
  2. Wait 24 hours.
  3. If no new booking, send email with rebooking link.
  4. Wait 72 hours.
  5. If still no booking, send final SMS reminder.
  6. If no action, tag as bd-lost.

11. Workflow D - Post-Consultation Whitening Upsell

Trigger:

Appointment Status = Completed

Actions:

  1. Move opportunity to Consultation Complete.
  2. Send same-day whitening follow-up email.
  3. Wait 2 days.
  4. If SMS consent is true, send whitening reminder SMS.
  5. Wait 3 days.
  6. Send final whitening follow-up email.
  7. If whitening appointment is booked, move to Whitening Booked.

12. Workflow E - Retention And Recall

Trigger:

Consultation complete or cleaning complete.

Actions:

  1. Wait 6 months.
  2. Add tag bd-recall-due.
  3. Send cleaning/check-up reminder email.
  4. Send SMS reminder if consent is true.
  5. If booked, update opportunity or create recall opportunity.

WEEKLY REVIEW

Diagnose before optimizing.

Decision cadence

  1. Check source completeness and delivery health.
  2. Use mature denominators for appointment outcomes.
  3. Find the first material drop in the journey.
  4. Change one variable and record the hypothesis.

Data-quality guardrails

  • Separate ad clicks from successfully loaded page views.
  • Deduplicate contacts and opportunities before counting leads.
  • Exclude future, cancelled, and rescheduled appointments from no-show denominators.
  • Report six-month recall only for contacts who have reached the due date.
MEASUREMENT BOUNDARY

Projected goals become real KPIs only after launch.

Until live traffic, sender delivery, compliance, and production tracking are active, the numbers above are targets to test against, not evidence to celebrate.
