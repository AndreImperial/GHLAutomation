Plain-English decoder

Marketing terms used on this site

Open any term for a short explanation and a Bloom Dental example.

Lead

A person who has shown interest and shared contact details. Here, a visitor becomes a lead after submitting the consultation form.

Funnel

The ordered steps that move someone toward a goal. This funnel runs from ad click to booked and attended consultation.

CTA

“Call to action”: the button or instruction that tells someone what to do next, such as “Request My Free Consultation.”

GHL / CRM

GoHighLevel is the customer-management tool used to store leads, manage appointments, and run automatic follow-up.

Pipeline

A visual list of each lead's current status, such as New Lead, Consultation Booked, No-Show, or Consultation Complete.

Workflow

An automatic if-this-then-that process. Example: when a form is submitted, add the lead, send an email, and share the booking link.

KPI

A number used to judge performance. The most important planned KPI here is cost per booked consultation.

Attach rate

The percentage of consultation patients who later choose whitening. It is a projected goal in this simulation, not an achieved result.

Project-making process

How the workshop became a launch-ready campaign system.

The process started with the business problem, then moved into campaign strategy, copy, funnel architecture, GHL workflow logic, and measurement. This section shows the thinking path, not just the finished files.

  1. 01
    Business Diagnosis

    I started by clarifying Bloom Dental Studio's current lead flow: about 20 website leads per month, 6 booked appointments, and a 35% no-show rate.

    Decision: the clinic did not mainly need more awareness; it needed faster follow-up, easier booking, reminders, and no-show recovery.
  2. 02
    Campaign Strategy

    I translated the business problem into a 60-day Meta-to-GHL campaign for busy BGC and Makati professionals who want smile guidance without pressure.

    Decision: lead with a free 30-minute consultation, then introduce PHP 15,000 whitening after trust is built.
  3. 03
    Conversion Content

    I wrote the landing page offer, FAQ, booking CTA, email confirmations, SMS reminders, no-show recovery, and whitening follow-up messages.

    Decision: reduce trust, time, and price concerns before asking the prospect to commit.
  4. 04
    GHL System Design

    I mapped Meta ad to landing page, inquiry form, booking calendar, confirmations, reminders, consultation, whitening upsell, and recall.

    Decision: specify pipeline stages, tags, custom fields, appointment triggers, and SMS consent rules before build.
  5. 05
    QA and Optimization Plan

    I defined checks for the page, form, calendar, templates, workflow branches, dashboard, test contact journey, no-show branch, and completed-appointment branch.

    Decision: review performance weekly by drop-off point: clicks, page conversion, booking rate, show rate, and whitening attach rate.

Step 01

I started by finding the bottleneck

The first move was to look at the simulated clinic situation like a strategist, not a designer. Bloom already had some inbound demand, so I treated the core problem as a lead handling and conversion system problem.

  • Reviewed the business, location, services, entry offer, upsell, and retention offer.
  • Identified the baseline lead flow: 20 leads, 6 bookings, and 35% no-shows.
  • Defined the main issue as manual follow-up, inconsistent reminders, and informal post-consultation follow-up.
Step 02

Then I chose the campaign mechanism

Instead of promoting whitening directly to cold traffic, I built the campaign around a free consultation. That made the funnel easier to enter and gave the clinic a trust-building step before the premium offer.

  • Set the simulated goal: 60 confirmed consultations in 60 days.
  • Chose Meta ads as the traffic source and GHL as the destination/system layer.
  • Positioned the offer around pressure-free smile guidance and clear next steps.
Step 03

Then I mapped the exact funnel path

Once the strategy was clear, I designed the patient journey as a sequence of conversion events. Every stage needed a job: capture, book, remind, recover, upsell, or recall.

  • Mapped Meta ad to landing page to inquiry form to calendar booking.
  • Defined the appointment reminder sequence and the consultation outcome branch.
  • Connected completed consultations to whitening follow-up and long-term recall.
Step 04

Then I wrote the conversion assets

The copy was created after the funnel map, so each message supported a specific point in the journey. The landing page handled the initial trust problem, while email and SMS handled attendance and follow-up.

  • Wrote the landing page hero, problem section, benefits, FAQ, pricing clarity, and CTA.
  • Wrote booking confirmation, 24-hour reminder, and 2-hour reminder messages.
  • Wrote no-show recovery and whitening follow-up messages for post-appointment movement.
Step 05

Then I implemented the GHL build

The project moved beyond planning when the strategy was translated into CRM objects and then configured in GHL: pipeline stages, tags, custom fields, form fields, calendar settings, triggers, waits, branches, and stop conditions.

  • Built the Free Consultation Funnel pipeline, stages, form, calendar, and landing page.
  • Configured tags and fields for lead state, booking state, whitening interest, consent, and recall.
  • Built workflows for new leads, reminders, no-show recovery, whitening follow-up, booking updates, and recall.
Step 06

Now I am making it measurable and testable

The remaining layer is dashboard setup and end-to-end QA. Since this is a simulation, this section shows exactly what will be checked without manufacturing live campaign results.

  • Created launch-readiness checks for page, form, calendar, templates, workflow branches, and dashboard.
  • Defined the North Star metric: cost per booked consultation.
  • Set weekly optimization logic for clicks, page conversion, booking rate, show rate, and attach rate.

Marketing Strategy Document: Bloom Dental Studio

1. Executive Summary

Bloom Dental Studio is a cosmetic and general dentistry clinic in Bonifacio Global City serving busy professionals in the BGC and Makati corridor. The clinic already receives some inbound interest, but its follow-up, booking, reminders, and post-consultation conversion process are too manual.

The primary campaign opportunity is to use GoHighLevel to turn interested prospects into booked consultations, reduce missed appointments, and create a structured path from consultation completion to Premium In-Chair Whitening revenue.

Primary campaign goal:

Book 60 confirmed free new-patient consultations within 60 days.

Supporting goals:

  • Reduce no-show rate from 35% to below 15%.
  • Convert 40% of completed consultations into Premium In-Chair Whitening bookings.
  • Build a repeatable GHL system for lead capture, appointment booking, reminders, no-show recovery, and post-consultation upsell.
2. Business Overview

Business:

Bloom Dental Studio

Location:

Bonifacio Global City, Metro Manila

Services:

  • General dentistry
  • Cosmetic dentistry
  • Teeth whitening
  • Veneers
  • Invisalign
  • Six-month cleaning and check-up

Entry offer:

Free 30-minute new-patient consultation.

Main upsell:

Premium In-Chair Whitening at PHP 15,000 per session.

Retention offer:

Six-month cleaning and check-up at PHP 3,500 per visit.

3. Target Audience

The primary audience is appearance-conscious professionals aged roughly 28-45 who work in BGC, Makati, finance, tech, corporate, and similar fields.

They are:

  • Busy and time-poor
  • Comfortable investing in appearance
  • Interested in a better smile
  • Cautious about price uncertainty
  • Sensitive to hard-selling
  • Likely to check reviews and proof before booking

Their main hesitation is not only money. It is also trust, time, and fear of pressure.

4. Current State

Current lead flow:

  • Around 20 website leads per month
  • Around 6 become booked appointments
  • Approximate lead-to-booking rate: 30%
  • No-show rate: 35%

Current problems:

  • Follow-up is handled manually by the front desk.
  • Leads can sit for one or two days before response.
  • Appointment reminders are inconsistent.
  • No-show recovery is not systemized.
  • Post-consultation whitening follow-up is informal.
  • Six-month recall is manual.
5. Strategic Diagnosis

Bloom Dental does not mainly have an awareness problem. It has a conversion and follow-up problem.

The system should therefore focus on:

  • Faster speed-to-lead
  • Easier booking
  • Stronger appointment reminders
  • Automated no-show recovery
  • Post-consultation whitening follow-up
  • Longer-term recall automation
6. Recommended Funnel Strategy

Use a dedicated GHL landing page connected to a GHL form and booking calendar.

The front-end offer should be the free 30-minute consultation, not whitening.

Why:

  • The consultation is lower friction for cold traffic.
  • It builds trust before the PHP 15,000 whitening offer.
  • It gives the clinic a chance to recommend treatment based on patient goals.
  • It creates a measurable conversion event in GHL.
7. Customer Journey
  1. Prospect sees Meta ad.
  2. Prospect visits GHL landing page.
  3. Prospect submits New Patient Inquiry form.
  4. Prospect books free consultation.
  5. GHL sends confirmation and reminders.
  6. Patient attends consultation.
  7. GHL sends whitening follow-up.
  8. Patient books whitening or enters future nurture.
  9. Patient receives six-month recall reminder later.
8. Positioning

Core message:

Get clear, pressure-free guidance on your smile options in one free 30-minute consultation.

Supporting angles:

  • Transparent next steps
  • No hard sell
  • Easy online booking
  • Designed for busy BGC/Makati professionals
  • Professional whitening available after consultation
9. KPIs

North Star metric:

Cost per booked consultation.

Primary KPIs:

  • Landing page conversion rate
  • Lead-to-booking rate
  • Booked consultations
  • No-show rate
  • Consultation completion rate
  • Whitening attach rate
  • Whitening revenue

Targets:

  • 60 booked consultations in 60 days
  • No-show rate below 15%
  • 40% whitening attach rate from completed consultations

8. Workflow A - New Lead To Booking

Trigger:

Form submitted: Bloom Dental - New Patient Inquiry

Actions:

  1. Add tag bd-new-lead.
  2. Add or update opportunity in New Lead.
  3. If service interest = Whitening, add tag bd-whitening-interest.
  4. Send email with booking link.
  5. If SMS consent is true, send SMS with booking link.
  6. Wait 24 hours.
  7. If no appointment is booked, send reminder to book.
9. Workflow B - Appointment Show-Up

Trigger:

Appointment Status = Booked

Filter:

Calendar = Bloom Dental - Free Consultation

Actions:

  1. Add tag bd-consult-booked.
  2. Move opportunity to Booked Consultation.
  3. Send booking confirmation SMS.
  4. Send booking confirmation email.
  5. Wait until 24 hours before appointment.
  6. Send 24-hour reminder email.
  7. Wait until 2 hours before appointment.
  8. Send 2-hour reminder SMS.
  9. Wait until after appointment time.
  10. Check appointment status.

If completed:

  • Move opportunity to Consultation Complete.
  • Add tag bd-consult-complete.
  • Start Workflow D - Post-Consultation Upsell.

Appointment Status = Completed

  4. Send SMS reminder if consent is true.
  5. If booked, update opportunity or create recall opportunity.

Glossary

GoHighLevel / GHL
A marketing CRM used to capture leads, book appointments, send messages, and move people through pipeline stages.
Funnel
The step-by-step path from seeing an ad to booking, attending, and receiving follow-up.
Lead
A person who showed interest by clicking, filling out a form, or asking for an appointment.
Pipeline
The CRM path that tracks lead status from New Lead to Whitening Paid or Lost.
Tag
A label used to trigger logic or mark behavior, such as SMS opt-in or no-show.
No-show recovery
A follow-up path that helps missed appointments rebook instead of disappearing.
Attach rate
The share of completed consultations that convert into whitening bookings.
North Star metric
The main metric for judging the campaign: cost per booked consultation.

Reflection

This project taught me to think about a campaign as an operating system, not a single asset. The landing page matters, but it only works when the offer, form, calendar, reminders, CRM stages, consent rules, recovery paths, and KPI loop are designed together.

The hardest part was keeping the simulation honest: the campaign needed targets and measurement logic, but it could not pretend to have real after-data. That is why the site separates designed future state from live performance.

Before a live launch, I would add verified clinic proof, finish sender configuration, pass every workflow branch with test contacts, connect Meta traffic, and replace projected targets with actual weekly performance data.

Portfolio positioning

A working proof piece for strategy, funnel thinking, and GHL implementation.

This project is strongest when presented as a system: business diagnosis, campaign plan, funnel blueprint, copy assets, implemented GHL objects, workflow logic, and measurement plan. That gives reviewers a clear view of both the thinking and the hands-on build, while keeping simulated targets separate from results.
